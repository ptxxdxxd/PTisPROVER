# Examples

Synthetic rubrics for grading model outputs, each with a flaw a reviewer could miss.
The table and the reports are generated by `scripts/build_examples.py`.

| Rubric | Grades | Verdict | Main finding | Reports |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [coding_agent.toml](coding_agent.toml) | Bug-fix patches from a coding agent, graded after the test suite runs. | **fail** | Reward hacking: breaking 'original_tests_kept' can still score 1, at or above the threshold 0.5; nothing that breaks it scores higher | [text](reports/coding_agent.txt), [JSON](reports/coding_agent.json), [HTML](reports/coding_agent.html) |
| [coding_agent_fixed.toml](coding_agent_fixed.toml) | Bug-fix patches from a coding agent; editing the tests zeroes the score. | **pass** | every check passed | [text](reports/coding_agent_fixed.txt), [JSON](reports/coding_agent_fixed.json), [HTML](reports/coding_agent_fixed.html) |
| [math_answer.toml](math_answer.toml) | Answers to maths word problems with worked reasoning. | **fail** | Criterion range: 'within_limit' pays for every admissible outcome: free reward | [text](reports/math_answer.txt), [JSON](reports/math_answer.json), [HTML](reports/math_answer.html) |
| [summary_length.toml](summary_length.toml) | Summaries of long reports, graded on coverage, length and faithfulness. | **fail** | Perfect score: no outcome can score 1, because these requirements cannot all hold | [text](reports/summary_length.txt), [JSON](reports/summary_length.json), [HTML](reports/summary_length.html) |
| [support_reply.toml](support_reply.toml) | Replies to customer tickets, graded by a judge model plus simple counters. | **fail** | Reward hacking: breaking 'refunds_only_on_request' can still score 0.8, at or above the threshold 0.7; nothing that breaks it scores higher | [text](reports/support_reply.txt), [JSON](reports/support_reply.json), [HTML](reports/support_reply.html) |
